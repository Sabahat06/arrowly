How to play

One simple rule. Endless puzzles.

You'll learn it in ten seconds – and still be thinking about it ten levels later.

Look at the board

Every arrow points somewhere. Spot which ones have a clear path to the edge.

Tap to slide it out

A free arrow glides off the board. A blocked one bumps back and costs a heart.

Clear every arrow

Empty the board to win. Fewer mistakes and a quick finish earn more stars and coins.

New mode

Tree Mode: think in branches

Arrows sit on a tree. Only the ones on free branches can leave, so you clear it from the leaves up – a brand-new kind of logic.

  • Up arrows are blocked by the arrows above them, down arrows by the ones below.
  • The tree re-balances smoothly after every move.
  • Trees grow bigger and trickier as you go.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is Arrowly free?

Yes. Arrowly is free to download and play. It is supported by ads. Rewarded ads are always optional – watch one only if you want an extra life, a free hint or bonus coins.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You can play every level offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads and to download small game updates such as reward settings.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no sign-in and no personal details. Your progress is saved on your device.

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before it ships, so there is always a way to clear the board. If you get stuck, use a hint or undo.

Which languages are supported?

English, Urdu, Hindi, Spanish and Portuguese.

What data does the app collect?

Your game progress stays on your phone. Google AdMob and Google Firebase may process device identifiers and anonymous usage and crash data to show ads and keep the game stable. Read the full Privacy Policy.
